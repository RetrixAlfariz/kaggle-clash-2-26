# bitrase-1.algo-6 neural sequence protocol

From-scratch PyTorch byte-CNN plus two-layer BiLSTM BIO classifier. Lowercased word vocabulary (maximum 50,000) and all character features are fit on prepared v1 train only. Random word embeddings are 128-dimensional; UTF-8 byte encoding is capped at 32 bytes, with 16-dimensional byte embeddings, width-3 convolution and 64 channels. The BiLSTM has hidden size 128 per direction, two layers, dropout 0.2, and 15 BIO outputs. AdamW uses learning rate 0.001, batch size 32, seed 2026, and eight epochs. CrossEntropy ignores padded tokens.

Dev is evaluated only at epochs 4 and 8. Raw argmax BIO spans and exact-K legal BIO decoding are both saved. Exact-K decoding uses `sequence_crf.decode_k` with zero transition weights; exposed dev entity counts are used only for this competition-aligned decoder. No pretrained weights, external data, holdout, or test data are used.

Pre-run root implementation details: CUDA training on the local RTX5060Ti16GB with PyTorch2.8.0+cu128, mixed precision float16 with GradScaler, gradient clipping1.0, AdamW weight_decay.01. Seven numeric flags preserve uppercase/digit/titlecase, left/right newline, and joined token boundaries. Batches are length-bucketed within deterministically shuffled blocks to reduce padding. Source/data hashes are frozen before encoding; checkpoints and predictions are retained at epochs4 and8. Best known-K dev checkpoint is selected, explicitly development selection rather than final holdout proof. Vocabulary uses train text only; train/dev unaligned entities are counted and dev gold is never dropped.
